How outstation pricing works

Outstation fares are calculated as billable kilometres (the greater of your actual distance or a minimum per-day billing floor) multiplied by the vehicle's per-km rate, plus a daily driver allowance. Tolls, parking and permits are shown separately as included or payable at actual — never bundled in silently.
